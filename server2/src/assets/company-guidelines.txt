Company Guidelines and Employee Handbook

Introduction

Welcome to ACME Innovations Private Limited. This document provides employees, contractors, interns, and consultants with a clear understanding of the company's expectations, standards, and best practices. These guidelines are intended to foster a productive, respectful, secure, and collaborative work environment. Every team member is expected to read, understand, and follow the policies described in this handbook.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build innovative technology solutions that improve business productivity while maintaining the highest standards of integrity, quality, and customer satisfaction. We strive to create products that solve real-world problems and establish long-term relationships with customers.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a globally recognized technology company known for innovation, reliability, and exceptional customer experience. We believe that sustainable growth comes from continuous learning, ethical business practices, and customer trust.

Core Values

Integrity
We act honestly, responsibly, and transparently in every interaction.

Innovation
We encourage creativity, experimentation, and continuous improvement.

Collaboration
Success is achieved through teamwork, mutual respect, and knowledge sharing.

Customer First
Customer satisfaction remains our highest priority in every decision.

Accountability
Every employee owns their responsibilities and commitments.

Learning
Continuous improvement and professional development are encouraged at every level.

Equal Opportunity

ACME Innovations provides equal employment opportunities regardless of race, gender, religion, nationality, disability, marital status, age, or any other legally protected characteristic. Every employee deserves a respectful and inclusive workplace.

Code of Conduct

Employees must behave professionally during all company-related activities.

Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect.

Avoid harassment, discrimination, bullying, or inappropriate language.

Protect confidential information.

Report unethical or illegal behavior immediately.

Avoid conflicts of interest.

Respect company property and equipment.

Comply with all applicable laws and regulations.

Professional Communication

Use respectful language in emails, meetings, chats, and documentation.

Avoid offensive jokes or inappropriate comments.

Respond to important communications within one business day whenever possible.

Use company-approved communication platforms.

Maintain professionalism during video meetings.

Working Hours

Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Flexible working hours may be available with manager approval.

Employees should:

Arrive on time.

Inform their manager if they will be late.

Record working hours accurately.

Attend required meetings.

Complete assigned tasks within agreed timelines.

Remote Work Policy

Employees approved for remote work must maintain a secure and distraction-free workspace.

Use company-issued devices whenever possible.

Connect through VPN when accessing internal systems.

Do not share company devices with family members.

Ensure a stable internet connection.

Attend scheduled virtual meetings.

Maintain availability during agreed working hours.

Attendance

Regular attendance is essential.

Repeated unexplained absences may result in disciplinary action.

Employees should notify their manager before taking leave whenever possible.

Data Security

Protecting company and customer information is everyone's responsibility.

Always use strong passwords.

Enable multi-factor authentication.

Lock your computer when away.

Never share passwords.

Encrypt confidential files.

Use approved cloud storage.

Avoid downloading unauthorized software.

Report suspicious emails immediately.

Information Classification

Information is classified into:

Public
Internal
Confidential
Restricted

Employees should only access information necessary for their job responsibilities.

Password Policy

Passwords must contain at least twelve characters.

Include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.

Do not reuse passwords across services.

Change passwords immediately if compromise is suspected.

Use an approved password manager.

Acceptable Use of Technology

Company computers are provided for business purposes.

Limited personal use is acceptable if it does not interfere with work.

Employees may not install unauthorized software.

Pirated software is prohibited.

Attempting to bypass security controls is prohibited.

Using company resources for illegal activities is prohibited.

Email Guidelines

Verify recipients before sending confidential information.

Avoid opening suspicious attachments.

Use professional language.

Do not send offensive or inappropriate content.

Archive important communications.

Physical Security

Wear identification badges while in company offices.

Challenge unknown visitors politely.

Report lost access cards immediately.

Do not allow unauthorized individuals into restricted areas.

Keep workspaces clean and secure.

Health and Safety

Follow workplace safety instructions.

Report hazards immediately.

Participate in emergency drills.

Know evacuation routes.

Maintain ergonomic workstations.

Take regular breaks during extended computer use.

Customer Privacy

Protect customer information.

Collect only necessary information.

Do not disclose customer information without authorization.

Follow applicable privacy regulations.

Delete data according to retention policies.
